💡 How to Use Ultimate Pro

🚀 Fill an entire year in minutes! Perfect for students, teachers, and professionals.

⚡ Quick Start (3 steps):

  1. Load a template - Choose from 9 lifestyle templates (athlete, student, parent, etc.)
  2. Apply to multiple days - Select days in the week/month view and click "Apply Current Day"
  3. Customize as needed - Click any time slot to change activities

🎯 Pro Tips:

  • Templates save automatically to the current day you're viewing
  • Switch resolutions (15/30/60 min) for different planning detail levels
  • Export your calendar to share with classmates or import to Google Calendar
  • Use reports to analyze how you spend your time across weeks/months
  • Create custom categories for activities specific to your lifestyle
